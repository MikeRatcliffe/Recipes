## description

This Chinese pork belly is meltingly tender with a sweet, spicy, and sticky coating. It uses a smart three-step process to achieve a perfectly crispy exterior before being tossed in a rich, caramelized glaze.

## ingredients

### Slow Cooked Pork Belly

- 1 kg rindless pork belly slices (chopped in half)
- 1 litre hot chicken stock
- 1 thumb-sized piece ginger (peeled and minced)
- 3 garlic cloves (peeled and chopped in half)
- 1 tbsp rice wine
- 1 tbsp caster sugar

### Sticky Glaze

- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 thumb-sized piece ginger (peeled and minced)
- 1 red chilli (finely chopped)
- 2 tbsp honey
- 2 tbsp light brown sugar
- 45 mL dark soy sauce
- 5 mL lemongrass paste
- Salt and pepper (a pinch)

### For Garnish

- Spring onions (chopped)
- Chillies (finely sliced)

## steps

### Simmer the Pork

1. Add the pork belly pieces, hot chicken stock, the first portion of minced ginger, garlic halves, rice wine, and caster sugar to a heavy-based pan.
2. Bring the mixture to a boil, cover with a lid, and simmer on low heat for 2 hours until the meat is melt-in-the-mouth tender.

### Prep the Pork & Glaze

1. Remove the pork from the liquid and drain it thoroughly. Pat dry using kitchen towels.
2. Slice the tender pork strips into bite-sized chunks.
3. In a separate small bowl, whisk together the sticky glaze ingredients: vegetable oil, minced ginger, chopped red chilli, honey, light brown sugar, dark soy sauce, and lemongrass paste.

### Fry and Glaze

1. Heat the remaining vegetable oil in a frying pan over high heat.
2. Toss in the pork chunks with a pinch of salt and pepper, frying until they turn crispy and golden brown.
3. Pour the sweet-spicy glaze over the pork. Continue cooking for a few minutes, tossing frequently, until the sauce reduces into a dark, shiny, sticky coating.
4. Remove from the heat, garnish with chopped spring onions and extra chillies, and serve warm.

## notes

### Gluten-Free Version

You can easily swap the dark soy sauce for tamari and double-check that your brand of chicken stock is certified gluten-free.

### Save the Liquid

Do not throw away the leftover boiling stock! It is packed with pork fat and aromatics, making it an incredible base for a Thai or Chinese noodle soup later on.
