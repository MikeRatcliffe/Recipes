## description

Toss the diced chicken directly in this blend along with a tiny splash of oil or melted butter to help it stick, or use it as a finishing sprinkle.

## ingredients

- 1 tbsp mild curry powder
- 1 tsp brown sugar
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp onion powder
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp ginger
- 1/4 tsp garam masala (optional)

## steps

1. Place your cooked diced chicken in a bowl.
2. Drizzle with 1 teaspoon of oil (like sesame or vegetable oil) just to make the surface slightly sticky.
3. Sprinkle the spice mix over the chicken and toss thoroughly until evenly coated.
