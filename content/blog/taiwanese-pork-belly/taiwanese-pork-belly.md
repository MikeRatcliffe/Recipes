## description

Rich, fragrant, and incredibly tender, this Taiwanese braised pork belly is pressure-cooked until the fat melts in your mouth. Served over a bed of freshly blanched lettuce to soak up the incredible gravy.

## ingredients

### Base & Aromatics

- Pork belly (cut into 4 cm pieces)
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 small onion (chopped)
- 3 large cloves minced garlic
- 1 thumb-sized piece ginger (sliced into 6-8 pieces)

### Liquids & Sauces

- 60 mL Shaoxing wine
- 1 tbsp regular soy sauce
- 1 tbsp dark soy sauce
- 2 tbsp oyster sauce
- 60 mL water

### Spices & Sweetener

- 2 tbsp brown sugar
- 2 whole star anise
- 1 cinnamon stick
- 1 bay leaf

### For Serving

- 1 head lettuce

## steps

### Saute the Aromatics & Pork

1. Turn on the saute mode on your pressure cooker and adjust to high (target temperature around 150C).
2. Add the vegetable oil to the pot and saute the chopped onion until it is nicely browned.
3. Move the onions off to the side of the pot. Add the pork belly pieces and saute for about 10 minutes to brown the meat, stirring occasionally.
4. In the last minute of sauteing, toss in the minced garlic and stir until fragrant.

### Add the Liquids & Spices

1. Pour in the Shaoxing wine, regular soy sauce, dark soy sauce, oyster sauce, and water. Stir everything together.
2. Turn off the saute mode.
3. Add the cinnamon stick, bay leaf, rock sugar, star anise, and ginger slices. Give the mixture a final stir.

### Pressure Cook

1. Place the lid on the pressure cooker and lock it into place, making sure the sealing valve is set to the locked position.
2. Set to manual/pressure cook on high pressure for 30 minutes.

### Skim the Fat & Thicken

1. Once cooking is complete and pressure is released, carefully open the lid.
2. Use a spoon to skim the rendered fat off the top of the sauce. Discard the fat properly into a jar or can (do not pour it down the sink).
3. Turn the saute mode back on and let the sauce simmer uncovered for about 5 minutes until it thickens and darkens. Turn off the cooker.

### Assemble & Serve

1. Blanch the whole head of green leaf lettuce for about 1 minute in salted water with a splash of oil. Drain well.
2. Arrange the blanched lettuce as a bed on your serving platter.
3. Spoon the pork belly and plenty of the gravy over the top of the lettuce, discarding the star anise, cinnamon stick, and bay leaf as you go.
4. Garnish with fresh chopped coriander and serve warm with a side of rice.
